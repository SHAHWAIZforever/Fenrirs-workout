Core Training Philosophy & Golden Rules

MANDATORY PROTOCOLS FOR 25 MINS OF HELL

HYDRATION RULE

At least 1 glass of water 30 minutes before your workout session.

INTENSITY RULE

Every single set go super intense! Each set should be to absolute failure trying to get another rep.

REST & CNS

Take rest days on CNS fatigue or muscle soreness, targeting sore body parts rather than full rest.

The 2-Set Method & Rest

  • Target reps: 8 reps per set.
  • If isometric hold: 1 full minute (excluding planche).
  • If you can't complete 8 reps, immediately regress to partials or easier regressions.
  • Rest time: 40-60 seconds between sets (tight 25 min duration).
