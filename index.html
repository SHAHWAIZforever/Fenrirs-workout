Core Training Philosophy & Golden Rules

Manual rules for every calisthenics session

Hydration Rule

At least 1 glass of water 30 minutes before your workout session.

Intensity Rule

Every single set go super intense! Each set should be to absolute failure trying to get another rep.

Rest Days & CNS

Take rest days on CNS fatigue or muscle soreness, but target particular sore body parts rather than full body rest.

The 2-Set Method & Rest

  • Target reps: 8 reps per set.
  • If isometric hold: 1 full minute (excluding planche).
  • If you can't complete 8 reps, immediately regress to partials or easier regressions to finish set reps.
  • Main goal: Hard progression every rep. If you hit 8 clean reps, move to a harder variation next time!
  • Rest time: 40-60 seconds between sets (due to the tight 25 min duration).
  • Note: Timers are there to stop you from doing too much, So the 25 mins is all it takes

Calorie & Nutrition Formulas

  • Obesity / Cut: 13 × 2.2 × kg
  • Normal Maintenance: 14 × 2.2 × kg
  • Workout Days: 15 × 2.2 × kg
  • Calories determine weight (drop 500 kcal for fat loss, add 500 kcal for mass).
  • Protein determines body composition. If tracking protein is hard, just track calories consistently!
